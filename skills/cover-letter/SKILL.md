# Cover letter

## Structure
1. Hook: two short, factual sentences on what I do and enjoy, tied to the company. No slogans.
2. Positioning: one paragraph with 2-3 keywords from the posting, role title in italics, and a one-line bridge into the examples.
3. Three or four proof points, each with a bold headline that carries a number, then action, method, result in about three lines.
4. Optional personal note (one sentence) only when a real connection exists.
5. Closing: one sentence that links my strengths to the role.

## Rules
- Facts only, from the CV, content library, story bank or my own words.
- Crisp means fewer words, not fewer facts: keep methods, dates and the functions involved.
- After a letter is sent, add its new opening, positioning or proof points to the letter library, labelled by company and role type.
- Skip the letter when the application does not ask for one.
