# Story bank

The story bank is the single source of truth for what I have done. CV bullets, letter proof points and form answers are all cut from it.

## Building a story
1. Start from my own words: a project description, a past CV bullet, a performance review or an interview answer.
2. Write it in STAR form: Situation, Task, Action (what I did, and what my team did, kept separate), Result (numbers and decisions leaders made).
3. Record the approved numbers exactly as I gave them, with their scope (e.g. "first year", "for traffic in scope"). Note numbers that must never be merged with other stories.
4. Tag each story by theme (negotiation, forecasting, AI adoption, reconciliation, stakeholder influence...) and by role family it suits.
5. List open questions instead of filling gaps: tools used, how actuals compared, who else was involved.

## Variants
For each story, keep short variants for different uses:
- a one-line CV bullet (verb first, scope then result, max 3 lines when rendered),
- a 3-line letter proof point with a bold headline that carries a number,
- a 100-250 word form or interview answer.

## Rules
- Facts only. If a detail would make the story stronger but I haven't confirmed it, ask.
- Keep partner and client names out of anything that leaves the private folder.
- When I correct a fact, update every variant of that story, not just the one in front of us.
