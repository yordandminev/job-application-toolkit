# Application answers

## Steps
1. Pick the story that answers the question most directly. Prefer a real story that is close to the question over a polished one that is off-topic, and say plainly when no source matches.
2. Draft in the order the question asks (e.g. assumptions, then how actuals compared). Lead with the result when the question allows.
3. Mark every missing fact as `[PLACEHOLDER]` and ask me for it. Never fill a gap with a plausible detail.
4. Keep partner and client names out unless I say otherwise.
5. Match the length the form allows; default to 100-150 words. When I ask for "more succinct", cut words, not facts.
6. Save the final answers in the role's fit analysis file.

## Voice
- Short sentences, one idea each; humble and factual; show traits through the story.
- Personality is welcome for "why us" questions (a light joke is fine); technical questions stay plain.
